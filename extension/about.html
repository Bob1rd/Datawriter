Datawriter By CUAI · Beta 0.5

Created by CU Amnesty International (CUAI), the Columbia University chapter of Amnesty International. Datawriter is a free browser extension built by student volunteers. It is not affiliated with trackdatacenters.com, whose public data it reads, or with any government body it lists, and it does not speak for Amnesty International as a whole.

What it is for

Large data centers are approved by local boards, often with little notice and under code names. Datawriter turns a ZIP code into the nearest tracked project, puts its size in everyday terms, names the officials who will decide, and writes a letter to each of them that opens in your own email. It saves the hours it takes to find the right official, the right facts and the right words.

Three steps

  1. Type your ZIP or town. Datawriter finds the nearest data center project on the tracker.
  2. See who decides: the local, county and state officials, with their published contacts.
  3. Press the envelope button beside an official, or the green bar under the project title. A letter opens in your email, already filled in. You press Send.

Where the information comes from

  • Projects, sizes and stages: trackdatacenters.com, read live on its own pages.
  • Officials, titles, emails and portraits: the governments' own websites, collected in October 2026 and dated in each record. Names found only in search results are left out. Where a 2026 name or title could not be confirmed, the panel says so. Party labels appear only where a source states them.
  • Comparisons: EIA, the Census Bureau, NEA, NCES, USDA, EPA, AHA and others, listed in full in the project's source list (docs/sources.md).
  • ZIP code locations: GeoNames (CC BY 4.0). Satellite imagery: Esri.

Privacy

Datawriter sends nothing on its own. Your name, address, email and phone stay in your browser's local storage and appear only in letters you choose to send. The only outside services it contacts are the tracker, Esri's map service for pictures, and a public ZIP lookup for ZIP codes outside the bundled states.
